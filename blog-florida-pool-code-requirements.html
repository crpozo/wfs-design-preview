What Florida Pool Code Actually Requires

The barrier rules people get wrong most often, and the fence systems that pass without a variance.

Pool Code Aluminum

Most failed pool inspections in Lee and Charlotte county come down to the same three things, and none of them are the fence panel itself.

The four foot minimum is a floor, not a target

Florida Building Code Section 454 sets a 48 inch minimum measured from the finished grade on the outside of the barrier. If your grade drops away from the pool, that measurement changes along the run.

  • Measure at the lowest point of the outside grade, not at the pool deck
  • A 4 ft panel on a slope can fall short at one end
  • Rackable panels follow grade without leaving a gap underneath

The gate is what actually fails

Panels rarely fail. Gates fail constantly, because self closing and self latching are two separate requirements and hardware is often specified for only one.

The latch has to sit at least 54 inches above grade, and the gate has to swing away from the pool.
